The journey

From Lebanon’s national team to NEOM — now Dubai

2007 – 2021

Notre Dame de Jamhour Club — Lebanon

Head tennis coach, advanced team manager and Physical & Sports Education lead — developing juniors from first rally to competitive play.

2015 – 2018

Lebanese Tennis Federation — National Team Coach

Coaching the Lebanese national tennis team and supporting the federation’s Junior Tennis Initiative in schools.

2017 – 2021

Sports Avenue Club — Managing Partner

Led club operations and grew the junior program exponentially while continuing to coach on court.

2021 – 2026

NEOM, Saudi Arabia — Head of Racquet Sports & Events

Directed racquet sports programs, competitive events and international training camps across NEOM communities.

2026 — Now

Dubai — Racquelier

Personalised coaching, padel socials and sport & wellness experiences for players of all levels across Dubai.
